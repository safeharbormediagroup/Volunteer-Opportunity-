# Volunteer-Opportunity-

​🎮 Volunteer Opportunity: Help Us Test Raven Rug Agua 3

​Safe Harbor Foundation × PULSE Technologies
​
We’re dropping a new Android game called Raven Rug Agua 3, and we need 12–20 college students to help us launch it on Google Play.

​What is Raven Rug?

​At its core, Raven Rug is an old-school fantasy RPG where you explore three kingdoms and complete quests. But underneath the gameplay, it’s built to solve a real-world problem: financial access.

​Most trading and market education is locked behind complex charts, heavy jargon, and expensive paywalls. 

Raven Rug breaks those macro-level trading concepts down into easy, micro-level choices.
You learn how market movements, risk, and resource management actually work—using in-game currency with zero real-world financial risk.

​Why it matters: We’re building this to give people in under-resourced communities—where access to financial gains, economic mobility, or even basic security like food access is tight

—a low-friction way to learn real trading mechanics. 

Understanding how money and trade work can literally open doors to financial freedom. 

We’re using gaming to level that playing field.

​What’s the Volunteer Gig?

​Google Play requires 12 opt-in testers to keep the app open for 14 straight days before it goes public.

​Over those 2 weeks, you'll:

​Download the test build from Google Play.
​
Tap in for a few minutes a day to play through levels and make sure it runs right.

​Catch bugs, test the controls, and give us your raw, honest feedback.

​Total time investment: 1–3 hours spread across the whole 2 weeks. 

Zero long sessions or complicated assignments.


​What’s in it for you?


​Verified Volunteer Hours: Get 1–3 hours logged for your school, org, or resume via GivePulse (NC A&T, UNCG, etc.) through Safe Harbor Foundation.

​Real Impact: Help test a tool designed to teach financial strategy to people who need it most.

​Support Local: Help get a locally built game out of closed testing and onto the official app store.

​Play a little, catch some bugs, and help launch something built for real community impact. 🚀

Disclaimer: individual gameplay directly helps train autonomous pilots designed to teach everyday inventory and asset management covering physical commodities to digital assets and Live market integrations are currently in development, game currency has no real-world cash value, and actual asset trading carries inherent financial risk.
